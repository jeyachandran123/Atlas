AI POS Vision System Development Roadmap

The Mindset

If I hire a new employee, I don't expect them to become an expert on the first day. I give them the basic instructions and let them start working. During their work, experienced cashiers correct their mistakes. Every correction becomes a lesson. Over time, that employee becomes highly accurate.

An AI Vision system should be built exactly the same way.

The goal is not to build a perfect AI before deployment.
The goal is to build a system that learns from real-world experience.

Phase 1 — Build the Product First (No Fine-tuning)

Don't think about training yet.

First build a working product.

Phase 3 — Analytics

Don't train immediately.

First ask:

What mistakes is AI making?

Chicken Biryani → Accuracy 98% → No problem.

Mini Meals → Accuracy 72% → Problem.

Special Meals → Accuracy 65% → Big problem.

Now ask why.

Reflection
Same color
Different lighting
Side angle
Bowl hidden

Now you know what to improve.

Phase 4 — Build the Dataset

This is an incredibly valuable dataset.

One Extra Step — Validation Before Training

AI Prediction
↓
Cashier Correction
↓
Was correction made?
↓
YES → Save for Training
NO → Optional Training Sample

Wrong predictions teach you the most.

If AI was already correct, that sample is still useful, but it is usually less informative than the mistakes.

Prioritize corrected samples when deciding what to review or include in future training rounds.

Final Philosophy

Don't try to build a perfect AI before users touch it.

Build a useful AI first.

Let real users and experienced cashiers teach it.

Collect corrections.

Create a high-quality dataset.

Fine-tune.

Deploy.

Repeat.

Just like a new employee becomes an expert through experience, your AI becomes an expert through real-world corrections.

The AI model is only the starting point.

Your continuously improving dataset becomes the real competitive advantage of your product.
