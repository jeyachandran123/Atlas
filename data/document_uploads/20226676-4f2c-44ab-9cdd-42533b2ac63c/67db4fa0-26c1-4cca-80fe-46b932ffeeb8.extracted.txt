UnityWorks Vision AI

Product & Technical Design Overview (Version 1.0)

Page 1 — Product Vision

UnityWorks Vision AI is a real-time Restaurant Monitoring Platform that transforms CCTV video into structured operational intelligence. Rather than functioning as a chatbot, the platform continuously observes restaurant activities and generates business events that integrate directly with the POS.

Business capabilities include:
• Employee Monitoring: PPE compliance (mask, gloves, hairnet, apron), hygiene violations and safety alerts.
• Customer Monitoring: Detection, tracking, seating status, waiting, eating, occupancy and analytics.
• Food Monitoring: Food recognition, menu mapping, portion estimation and finished plate detection.
• Table Monitoring: Occupied, empty, dirty, clean and ready-for-cleaning status.
• Restaurant Analytics: Occupancy trends, peak hours, hygiene score and employee compliance.

Engineering Philosophy:
The product never tries to solve everything with one AI model. Instead, each layer has one responsibility, producing reliable, explainable and scalable outputs.

Layer Summary

OpenCV – Reads RTSP streams and extracts frames. We intentionally analyse around 1 FPS initially because restaurant scenes change relatively slowly, reducing GPU cost dramatically.

YOLO – Detects objects and produces bounding boxes with confidence scores. It localizes people, plates, tables and other objects before any reasoning occurs.

ByteTrack – Maintains persistent identities across frames. Employee #4 remains Employee #4 rather than becoming a new person every second.

Object Cropper – Crops only the detected object and sends that crop to Vision AI. This improves accuracy while reducing latency and token usage.

Object Classification – Uses multiple signals (location, attire, kitchen zone, badge, repeated appearance, etc.) to classify Employee, Customer, Plate or Table.

Business Rules Engine is the heart of the platform.

The AI should never directly claim 'Customer is eating Chicken Biryani.'

Instead the system combines:
Customer Tracking
+
Table Mapping
+
Plate Detection
+
Food Recognition
+
Temporal Tracking
=
Business Inference

Example:
Customer #12 sits at Table 8.
Plate #22 is recognised as Chicken Biryani.
The tracked customer repeatedly interacts with Plate #22 and the food quantity decreases.
Business Rule → 'Customer #12 is likely eating Chicken Biryani.'

This separation makes every decision explainable and reduces hallucinations.

Page 4 — Restaurant State, Deployment & Learning

Instead of treating every frame independently, UnityWorks Vision AI maintains a live Restaurant State.

Restaurant State
• Employee States
• Customer States
• Table States
• Food States

Every new frame updates this state. Only meaningful state changes create events such as:
• Employee removed mask.
• Table became occupied.
• Food finished.
• Table ready for cleaning.

Page 5 — Engineering Principles

Core Engineering Principles

1. Detect first, reason later.
2. Never send the whole frame to the Vision LLM.
3. Every architectural layer has exactly one responsibility.
4. Track objects before making business decisions.
5. Business Rules convert AI observations into operational facts.
6. Maintain a Restaurant State instead of analysing isolated frames.
7. Generate events, not conversations.
8. Use confidence thresholds and sustained validation before raising alerts.
9. Build independent modules that can evolve without affecting the entire system.
10. Human feedback is part of the product, enabling continuous learning and future fine-tuning.

This architecture provides a scalable foundation that starts with a single restaurant and naturally expands to multi-branch deployments without redesigning the core platform.
